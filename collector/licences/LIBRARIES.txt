Libraries in tools/lhm/
=======================

The collector reads CPU temperature with LibreHardwareMonitorLib on
.NET Framework 4.7.2. These are the DLLs it needs, each taken unmodified from
the official NuGet package named below (https://www.nuget.org/packages/<id>/<version>).
The SHA-256 of every package and of every DLL is pinned in
temperature-tools.json and checked when the zip is built.
